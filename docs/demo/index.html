Interactive demo

Try Life in the UK Test Prep in your browser

This is a working preview of the Android app. Answer real practice questions, see the explanations, open the study notes and take a short timed mock test. Press Play tour for a guided walkthrough.

The demo uses 13 sample questions and a 6-question mock with a 6-minute timer. The app has over 770 practice questions and full 24-question, 45-minute mock tests. Open the demo full screen.

What to try

Five things to try in the demo

  1. Get a question wrong

    Open Practice, pick a chapter and choose a wrong answer on purpose. You see your answer, the correct one and why.

  2. Read about it

    Tap "Read about this" to open the study note for that section, with the fact you missed at the top.

  3. Check your plan

    Go back to Home. Your plan for today now starts with the section you found hardest.

  4. Take a mock test

    Start a mock from the Mocks tab. The timer runs and you get no feedback until you finish.

  5. See your progress

    Your readiness score and chapter mastery update as you answer.
