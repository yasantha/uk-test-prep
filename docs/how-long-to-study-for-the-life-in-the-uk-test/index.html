How long to study for the Life in the UK Test

There is no official figure, but most people need two to four weeks of short daily sessions.

A realistic timeline

Your situationSuggested timeDaily study
Strong English, some knowledge of the UK2 weeks30 to 45 minutes
Strong English, new to UK history and culture3 to 4 weeks20 to 30 minutes
English is a second language4 to 6 weeks30 minutes, with read-aloud

These are guides, not rules. What matters is that you score consistently high in timed mock tests.

A 2-week plan

  1. Days 1 to 3: chapters 1 and 2, then questions.
  2. Days 4 to 8: chapter 3 (history) one period per day.
  3. Days 9 to 11: chapters 4 and 5.
  4. Days 12 to 14: a full mock test each day, then review every wrong answer.

How to know you are ready

You are ready when you score 21 or more out of 24 in at least three timed mock tests in a row. A 75% pass mark leaves little room for a bad day, so a margin helps.

Tips that save time

  • Study a little every day instead of one long weekend session.
  • Spend more time on chapter 3, which is the longest and has the most questions that catch people out.
  • Use questions as study tools. Read the explanation for each one you get wrong.

The full 4-week plan is in our study guide.

Quick questions

How many weeks does it take to prepare for the Life in the UK Test?

Most people need two to four weeks of 20 to 45 minutes a day. People studying in a second language may need longer.

How do I know I am ready for the test?

When you score 21 or more out of 24 in several timed mock tests in a row.
