About the test

What is the Life in the UK Test?

Most people applying for settlement (indefinite leave to remain) or British citizenship must pass the Life in the UK Test. It checks what you know about British values, history, traditions and everyday life.

The test has 24 multiple-choice questions. You have 45 minutes and need at least 18 correct answers (75%) to pass. The questions come from the official handbook, Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents.

Book your test and check the latest rules on GOV.UK. Read our study guide for a 4-week plan, or try some free practice questions.

The five chapters of the handbook

  1. 1The values and principles of the UKRead for background. The app keeps it as study notes.
  2. 2What is the UK?The countries, capitals and Crown dependencies
  3. 3A long and illustrious historyFrom the Stone Age to modern Britain
  4. 4A modern, thriving societyReligion, customs, sport, arts and places
  5. 5The UK government, the law and your roleParliament, courts, rights and responsibilities

Questions

Common questions

Is this the official Life in the UK Test app?

No. Life in the UK Test Prep is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Home Office or the UK Government. The questions are written to help you study the official handbook and are not the questions used in the real test.

Does the app work without the internet?

Yes. All questions, notes and mock tests are stored on your phone, so you can study offline. An internet connection is only needed for optional backup, subscriptions and ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress stays on your phone. You can sign in with Google if you want to back up your progress and restore it on a new phone.

How is my readiness score worked out?

It combines your recent mock test results with how many questions you have mastered. Aim to keep it above the 75% pass mark before your test.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. The app is available for Android phones on Google Play.
