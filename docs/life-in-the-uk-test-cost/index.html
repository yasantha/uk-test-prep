How much does the Life in the UK Test cost?

The test fee is £50, paid when you book on GOV.UK. Here is when you can get it back and how to avoid paying twice.

£50test fee
3 daysminimum notice to book
72 hoursnotice to cancel for a refund

The fee

It costs £50 to book the Life in the UK Test. You pay online when you book through the official GOV.UK Life in the UK Test page. If you need to take the test again, you pay the £50 fee again each time. Fees can change, so always check the current amount on GOV.UK before you book.

Book only on GOV.UK

Some websites offer to book the test for you or charge a "booking fee". You do not need them. The official booking service is on GOV.UK, and booking there costs only the test fee.

Cancelling and refunds

  • Cancel at least 72 hours (3 days) before your test and you get a refund to the card you paid with.
  • If you cancel or rearrange your test less than 3 days before it, you do not get a refund.
  • If the test centre cancels, you can ask for a refund within 3 months of the test date.

When you will not get your money back

GOV.UK says you will not get a refund if you cannot take the test because:

  • you bring the wrong identification, or the name on your booking does not exactly match your ID
  • you arrive late or do not turn up
  • you are ill on the day
  • you refuse to have your photo taken at the centre

Double-check your ID and booking name against our booking and what to bring guide before the day.

The cheapest way to pass: once

Every failed attempt costs another £50. Practise with timed mock tests until you score 21 or more out of 24 three times in a row, then book. Start with our free practice questions.

Quick questions

How much does the Life in the UK Test cost?

£50, paid when you book on GOV.UK. Check GOV.UK for the current fee.

Do I pay again if I fail?

Yes. You pay the £50 fee each time you book the test.

Can I get a refund on the Life in the UK Test?

Yes, if you cancel at least 72 hours before the test, or if the test centre cancels. Not if you cancel later, bring the wrong ID, arrive late or are ill.
