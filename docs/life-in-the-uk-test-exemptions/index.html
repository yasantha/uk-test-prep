Life in the UK Test exemptions: who does not need to take it

Most adults applying for settlement or citizenship must pass the test, but some people do not need to take it.

You do not need to take the test if you are

  • under 18
  • 65 or over
  • someone who has already passed it, for example when you applied for settlement
  • someone with a long-term physical or mental condition that stops you taking it

Exemption for a long-term condition

If a long-term physical or mental condition means you cannot take the test, you must send evidence with your application. GOV.UK says this should be either an exemption form or a letter from a doctor confirming your condition. Check the current form and guidance on the GOV.UK Life in the UK Test page.

Already passed? You do not take it again

If you passed the test for indefinite leave to remain, you do not need to sit it again when you apply for British citizenship. Use the unique reference number you got when you passed. Read more about your result and reference number.

Need help to take the test instead?

Being exempt is different from needing support. If you have a disability, you can ask for reasonable adjustments when you book, such as extra equipment or help getting into the test centre.

Other requirements still apply

An exemption from the Life in the UK Test does not automatically exempt you from other requirements, such as showing your knowledge of English. Each route has its own rules, so check GOV.UK or ask an immigration adviser regulated in the UK.

Quick questions

Who is exempt from the Life in the UK Test?

People under 18, people aged 65 or over, people who have already passed it, and people with a long-term physical or mental condition who provide an exemption form or a doctor's letter.

Do I need to take the test again for citizenship if I passed for ILR?

No. If you have already passed, you use the same pass and do not take it again.

Can I get help to take the test if I have a disability?

Yes. You can ask for reasonable adjustments, such as extra equipment or help accessing the centre, when you book.
