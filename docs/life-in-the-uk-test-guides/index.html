Life in the UK Test guides

Short guides to each handbook chapter and to the test itself: format, booking, retakes and how long to study.

  • Chapter 1: The values and principles of the UKChapter 1 is short and sets out the values the rest of the handbook builds on. Here is what it says and how to use it.
  • Chapter 2: What is the UK?Chapter 2 covers the geography and make-up of the UK. It is short, and the questions are very learnable.
  • Chapter 3: A long and illustrious historyHistory is the longest chapter and where most people lose marks. Learn it as a timeline, not as separate dates.
  • Chapter 4: A modern, thriving societyChapter 4 describes everyday life, traditions and culture. Dates of festivals and famous names are the main things to learn.
  • Chapter 5: The UK government, the law and your roleChapter 5 explains how the UK is governed and what is expected of residents and citizens. The facts are precise, so learn the numbers.
  • Pass mark and formatThe short answer: 24 questions, 45 minutes, and you need 18 correct to pass.
  • Booking and what to bringBook through the official GOV.UK service only. Here is how it works and how to prepare for the day.
  • Retake rulesFailing is common and not the end. You can take the test again. Here is what to do.
  • ILR and citizenshipMost adults who apply for settlement or British citizenship must pass the test. Here is who needs it and who does not.
  • How long to studyThere is no official figure, but most people need two to four weeks of short daily sessions.
