Guide

Life in the UK Test results and your unique reference number

You need 75% to pass. If you pass, you get a unique reference number, which you need for your application.

Getting your result

The pass mark is 75%, which means at least 18 of the 24 questions correct. You find out your result at the test centre after you finish.

Your unique reference number

When you pass, you get a unique reference number. You need this number when you apply for indefinite leave to remain (settlement) or British citizenship, so keep it somewhere safe, such as a note on your phone and a printed copy at home.

If you passed before 17 December 2019, you were given a pass notification letter with a test reference ID instead. You use that letter in your application.

One pass, two applications

If you passed when you applied for settlement, you do not need to take the test again for citizenship. Use the same pass. See who else does not need to sit the test on our exemptions page.

What if I lose my pass letter?

If you have lost your pass notification letter, GOV.UK says to write a letter explaining this and send it with your citizenship or settlement application. Include as many details of your test as you can, such as the date and the test centre.

If you did not pass

You can book again and pay the fee again. Use your result to find your weak areas. Our retake guide explains a plan for the next attempt.

Quick questions

When do I get my Life in the UK Test result?

At the test centre, after you finish the test.

What is the Life in the UK Test unique reference number?

The number you get when you pass. You need it for your settlement or citizenship application.

What if I lost my Life in the UK Test pass letter?

Write a letter explaining that you lost it and send it with your application, as GOV.UK advises.
