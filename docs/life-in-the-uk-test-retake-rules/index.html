Failed the Life in the UK Test? Retake rules and next steps

Failing is common and not the end. You can take the test again. Here is what to do.

Can I retake the test?

Yes. You can retake the Life in the UK Test as many times as you need. You must book and pay the £50 fee again each time, and you must book at least 3 days before the test. GOV.UK does not set a separate waiting period between attempts, but rules can change, so check the GOV.UK Life in the UK Test page before you rebook. Read our guide to the test cost, refunds and rebooking.

How to improve before the next attempt

  1. Find your weak chapter. Most people lose marks in history (chapter 3). Check which chapter your wrong answers came from.
  2. Study the explanations. For every question you miss, read why the right answer is right.
  3. Re-test the same questions. Come back to missed questions after a day, then after a week.
  4. Take timed mocks. Practise 24 questions in 45 minutes until you score 21 or more three times in a row.
  5. Book when ready. You do not have to book the earliest possible date.

Common reasons people fail

  • Trying to memorise dates without understanding the order of events.
  • Mixing up the UK, Great Britain and the British Isles.
  • Missing the "choose two answers" questions.
  • Not taking full mock tests under timed conditions.

Quick questions

Can I retake the Life in the UK Test if I fail?

Yes, as many times as needed. You book and pay again each time.

How long must I wait to retake the test?

GOV.UK does not set a waiting period between attempts, but every booking must be made at least 3 days before the test, and you pay the £50 fee each time.
